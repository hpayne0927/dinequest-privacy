Dine Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Dine Quest has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads and no analytics. We don't run a server, so we never receive your data.

Location

If you allow location access, Dine Quest uses your device's location only while you're using the app, to find places to eat and drink near you. To do that, your approximate search area (coordinates and a radius) is sent to:

  • OpenStreetMap's public Overpass servers (overpass-api.de and overpass.openstreetmap.fr), which return the list of nearby places.
  • Apple MapKit, which shows maps and place details such as photos, ratings and hours.

These requests contain no name, email, account or device identifier from Dine Quest. Like any web request, the servers can see your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply. If you don't allow location access, you can still search any city by name.

Data that stays on your device

Your food log (visits, ratings, notes and photos), preferences and reminders are stored only on your iPhone. They're never sent to us or anyone else. Photos you add come from the system photo picker, which only gives Dine Quest the pictures you choose. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Notifications

Meal and "second chance" reminders are scheduled on your device. No push service or server is involved.

Websites and other apps

Restaurant menus, websites and recipe searches (Allrecipes) open inside the app in a web view. DoorDash, Apple Maps and Google Maps open in their own apps or in Safari. Those sites and services handle your data under their own privacy policies.

Tracking

Dine Quest doesn't track you across apps or websites. It contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code.

Children

Dine Quest doesn't knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
